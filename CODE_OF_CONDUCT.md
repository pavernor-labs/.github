# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be direct about the work and generous with the person. Disagree with the idea,
not the human.

## Expected

- Critique code, claims, and evidence — precisely, and with reasons.
- Assume competence. Ask what you are missing before assuming a mistake.
- Accept that "I was wrong" is a normal sentence here.

## Not tolerated

- Harassment, slurs, or personal attacks.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without consent.

## Scope

Applies in repositories, issues, pull requests, and any space where you are
representing Pavernor Labs.

## Enforcement

Report to **security@pavernor.tech**. Reports are handled privately. Responses
range from a warning to a permanent ban, depending on severity and pattern.

This is deliberately short. Contributor Covenant 2.1 applies for anything not
covered here.
